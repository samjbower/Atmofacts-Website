## Press Release

**LONGMONT, Colo.** — AtmoFacts, a spatial-analytics company transforming atmospheric measurements into actionable environmental intelligence, announced a major expansion in accessibility and reach for its flagship FluxMapper technology through LI-COR Cloud. This collaboration enables a new level of convenience and scalability for flux-tower users worldwide while preserving AtmoFacts' direct pathways for advanced scientific and operational use cases.

FluxMapper represents a step-change in what can be learned from every flux tower, revealing where heat, water, and carbon fluxes originate across heterogeneous landscapes. By turning a single tower into tens of thousands of virtual sensors, FluxMapper scales trusted LI-COR measurements with modern spatial analytics, delivering audit-ready FluxMaps™ that provide transparent attribution, high-resolution diagnostics, and next-generation insights for land management, Measurement, Reporting and Verification (MRV), and environmental forecasting.

"FluxMapper has always been about democratizing access to atmospheric insight," said Stefan Metzger, Founder and CEO of AtmoFacts. "Through LI-COR Cloud, that capability becomes dramatically more accessible—bringing flux measurements into the 21st century by letting every molecule meet its map and making environmental performance as measurable as any business metric."

"FluxMapper brings new precision to carbon accounting by clearly showing where carbon is emitted and where it's being stored," said Tom Reslewic, CEO of LI-COR. "Integrated into LI-COR Cloud, it gives customers real-time insights into how land-management decisions and other factors affect sequestration and emissions—powerful information for those committed to monitoring carbon globally."

### Accessible Today, Evolving Tomorrow: Early Cohort at AGU25

FluxMapper will appear in LI-COR Cloud through a staged rollout. At AGU25, AtmoFacts and LI-COR will open nominations for initial early-access cohorts. Selected participants will benefit from early access to the integrated workflow, concierge onboarding, and the opportunity to inform the integration with their own sites and data. Reach out to nominate for the early cohort with [this form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSftvjB3XqbL1wcF5lBeWxozI1byjF-Y-Rr3uuhsWUdxM-3eJQ/viewform).

### A Dual-Path Access Model: Convenience via LI-COR Cloud, Advanced Analytics via AtmoFacts

With LI-COR Cloud as a user-friendly entry point and AtmoFacts continuing to independently support advanced science, non-standard deployments, next-generation analytics, and API-based workflows, FluxMapper now serves the full range of atmospheric-measurement users—from routine tower operations to research networks, MRV developers, and remote-sensing groups.

Interested parties can direct inquiries to [info@atmofacts.com](mailto:info@atmofacts.com) and experience FluxMapper in the LI-COR Cloud at its AGU25 debut. Technical demonstrations, case studies, and cohort registration are available during the conference at LI-COR booth #311.

### About AtmoFacts

AtmoFacts™ is a spatial-analytics company that transforms atmospheric measurements into actionable environmental intelligence. Its flagship product, FluxMapper™, spatializes eddy-covariance data into high-resolution FluxMaps™ that quantify heat, water, and carbon exchange across heterogeneous landscapes. By turning a single tower into ~90,000 virtual sensors, FluxMapper delivers transparent, audit-ready flux attribution linked to land management and property boundaries. AtmoFacts' patented spatial engine—now accessible through LI-COR Cloud—supports scientific research, environmental stewardship, agriculture, carbon-market MRV, and next-generation remote-sensing calibration. Learn more at [atmofacts.com](https://www.atmofacts.com).
