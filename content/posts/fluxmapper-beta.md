---
title: "FluxMapper™ On-Demand beta is live"
date: "2025-06-15"
description: "Run spatialized eddy-covariance workflows directly from your browser."
---
FluxMapper™ is now available through an on-demand web interface connected to the
NEON tower network. Choose a subset of raw data, choose a NEON site, and let our
cloud resources do the heavy lifting. Early partners are using the beta to test
MRV strategies and to validate methane inspections.

The beta program will remain open through AGU. Send feedback via the form on the beta web page and help us prioritize the next set of features.
